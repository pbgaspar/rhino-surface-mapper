Qt 6.11.2 runtime footprint
---------------------------
The validated bundle contains QtCore, QtGui, QtNetwork, QtOpenGL,
QtOpenGLWidgets, QtPdf, QtQml, QtQmlMeta, QtQmlModels, QtQmlWorkerScript,
QtQuick, QtSvg, QtUiTools, QtVirtualKeyboard, and QtWidgets. It also contains
the Windows platform, image-format, TLS, SVG icon-engine, style, touch,
virtual-keyboard input-context, and network-information plugins. Qt Virtual
Keyboard is distributed under GPLv3 on the chosen open-source route. Qt PDF
is distributed under its applicable GPLv3/LGPLv3 open-source terms; Qt
WebEngine is not bundled.

The complete attribution articles from the upstream Qt 6.11.2 pages for the
applicable embedded components are consolidated in
LICENSES/Qt-6.11.2-Third-Party-Notices.html. The document identifies each
original page and contains its component copyright notices and licence terms.

The mapped components are:

- Qt Core: Apache Tika MIME definitions, BLAKE2, zlib, Robert Penner easing
  equations, double-conversion, MD4, MD5, PCRE2 and SLJIT, SHA-1, SHA-3,
  SHA-384/SHA-512, SipHash, TinyCBOR, Unicode UCD, Unicode CLDR, and
  tl::expected. These are incorporated by Qt6Core.dll.
- Qt GUI/OpenGL/Widgets: Adobe Glyph List, FreeType (including its rasterizer,
  BDF/PCF support and zlib code), D3D12 Memory Allocator and mipmap code,
  Emoji Segmenter, HarfBuzz, libjpeg-turbo, libpng, MD4C, OpenGL/OpenGL ES
  headers, Pixman, smooth-scaling code, Vulkan registry and memory allocator,
  WebGradients, Wintab, and the embedded sRGB profile. These correspond to the
  shipped Qt GUI/OpenGL/Widgets libraries and Windows platform/style plugins.
- Qt Image Formats: libtiff and libwebp, corresponding to the shipped qtiff
  and qwebp plugins.
- Qt Network: Public Suffix List and libpsl, incorporated by Qt6Network.dll.
- Qt QML/Quick: JavaScriptCore Macro Assembler and Yoga, incorporated by the
  shipped Qt QML and Qt Quick libraries.
- Qt SVG: XSVG, incorporated by Qt6Svg.dll and the SVG plugins.
- Qt Virtual Keyboard: OpenWnn, PinyinIME, and Traditional Chinese IME,
  corresponding to Qt6VirtualKeyboard.dll and its input-context plugin.
- Qt PDF: Abseil, FreeType, PDFium, Chromium-origin code, fast_float, ICU,
  libjpeg-turbo, libpng, and zlib, incorporated by Qt6Pdf.dll and qpdf.dll.
  This does not imply that Qt WebEngine is distributed.
- Windows software OpenGL fallback: Mesa llvmpipe and its Boost-licensed C11
  threads emulation, corresponding to opengl32sw.dll.

The shared Apache License 2.0 text is also provided as
LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt. Component-specific alternatives and attributions
